    Do this once per sensor

    Getting started

    1. Switch on the controller. Within 10 seconds it creates a Wi-Fi hotspot called SolarIrrigation.
    2. On your phone, join that Wi-Fi (password irrigate123 unless it was changed). Ignore the "no internet" message.
    3. Open a browser and go to http://192.168.4.1 or http://irrigation.local. Add it to your home screen for one-tap access.
    4. The pill at the top right shows Live when data comes from the controller. Demo means you are viewing simulated data.
    5. The first time, check Setup → Crop, calibrate each soil sensor (Tracker & sensors tab) and press Set clock from this phone if the clock shows "not set".

    How automatic watering works

    The controller runs this cycle every second. It waters only as much as the crop needs, when it needs it.

    1. Measure. Read every soil sensor, ignore any that are disconnected, and average the rest. One faulty sensor can't flood or dry out the field.
    2. Decide. If the average is below the start level and it is a watering hour, start a cycle. Between start and target nothing changes. This gap stops the pump from switching on and off all the time.
    3. Choose the speed. The drier the soil, the faster the pump. At or below the critical level it runs at 100 %. Close to the target it slows to the lowest speed. This saves battery and avoids high pressure and runoff.
    4. Pulse and soak. After the run time (default 5 min) the pump pauses (default 10 min) so water sinks to the roots before the sensor is read again. This gives deeper roots and less waste.
    5. Stop at target. When the soil reaches the target level the pump stops and the cycle ends.
    6. Protect. The pump is blocked if the battery is low, if the daily water limit is reached, or if the soil does not get wetter while pumping (empty tank or broken pipe). Each event is written to the activity log.

    Auto or manual?

    • Auto (recommended for every day). The controller waters by itself. You can still press Run pump for an extra timed watering. If you press Stop, auto watering pauses for 30 minutes, or until you press Resume auto.
    • Manual. The pump runs only when you press Run pump, and stops after the time you choose. Use it when testing, flushing pipes, applying fertiliser through the water, or if a sensor is broken.
    • Low-battery protection and the run-time limit stay on in both modes.

    Care and safety

    • Weekly: wipe dust off the solar panel, clean the pump inlet filter, check drip lines for blockages.
    • Monthly: check battery terminals for corrosion, check cable glands on the box are tight, compare sensor readings with a hand squeeze test.
    • Disconnect the battery before touching wiring. Keep the control box closed and shaded.
    • Never run the pump dry. Keep the tank inlet below the water level.
    • Keep children away from the panel tracker while it moves.
